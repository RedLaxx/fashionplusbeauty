Plaid is back on everyone's fall mood board, and the sweater is the easiest way to wear it. One plaid sweater gives you pattern and warmth in a single piece, so you do not have to build an outfit around a loud shirt or a matching set. Pinterest searches for plaid sweater styles have jumped sharply this season, which suggests a lot of people are asking the same question: what do I actually wear with it?

## Before you buy: how to pick a plaid sweater

A few small choices decide whether a plaid sweater becomes a favorite or a piece that sits in the drawer.

Start with scale. Small checks read almost like a solid from a distance, so they go with nearly everything. Large, bold plaids make a statement, which means the rest of the outfit should stay quiet.

Next, look at the colors inside the plaid. Choose one that shares at least one shade with clothes you already own. A brown and cream plaid works with denim, black and camel. A red and navy plaid works with jeans and white. If you cannot name three things in your closet that match, keep looking.

Fit matters just as much. Oversized sweaters look best with slim or straight bottoms, so the outfit does not turn into one big shape. Cropped cardigans suit high-waisted jeans and trousers. Fitted knits work well when you want to tuck the sweater into a skirt.

Finally, think about the knit. A chunky knit softens plaid and feels casual. A fine merino knit makes the same pattern look sharper, which is what you want for the office.

## 15 plaid sweater outfits for fall

### 1. Red plaid sweater with dark jeans and ankle boots

This is the classic, and it needs almost no planning. A red and black plaid sweater with dark straight-leg jeans already looks finished. Black ankle boots pick up the black in the pattern, and a plain black belt or bag completes it. Keep jewelry small. Red plaid is warm enough to take silver or gold. Wear this one to pumpkin patches, coffee runs and school pickup, anywhere you want to look put together without looking dressed up.

### 2. Plaid cardigan with a white tee and straight jeans

A plaid cardigan is easier to wear than a pullover because you can leave it open, button it halfway or drape it over your shoulders. Underneath, a plain white tee and light-wash straight jeans keep things clean. Brown loafers and white leather sneakers both work here. This is the cardigan sweater outfit to reach for on days that start cold and end warm, since you can take the cardigan off without losing the look.

![Same woman in an open grey-and-cream plaid cardigan over a white tee, light-wash straight jeans, white leather sneakers and a small crossbody bag, walking under autumn trees](/images/plaid-sweater-outfits-03-outfit-02-grey-cardigan-jeans.jpg)

### 3. Camel and cream plaid sweater with brown trousers

If bold color is not your thing, a neutral plaid is the better choice. A camel and cream check sweater with chocolate brown wide-leg trousers stays in one warm color family, and that is why it looks expensive. Add pointed flats and a tan tote. Gold jewelry suits this palette well. The look works for a long lunch, a gallery visit or a workday when you want to feel relaxed and polished at once.

![Same woman in a camel-and-cream plaid knit sweater, chocolate brown wide-leg trousers and pointed tan flats, carrying a tan tote in a minimalist apartment](/images/plaid-sweater-outfits-04-outfit-03-camel-trousers.jpg)

### 4. Plaid sweater over a white collared shirt

The preppy version. Let a crisp white collar and cuffs show under a crew-neck plaid sweater, then add dark jeans or navy trousers and loafers. If the sweater is long, tuck the shirt tails in so the waist stays defined. Navy and green plaids look especially good with a white collar. It is a reliable choice for casual office days, campus, or any plan that calls for neat but not formal.

### 5. Plaid sweater with faux leather leggings

Soft knit against a shiny, sleek bottom is a good contrast. Wear a chunky plaid sweater with black faux leather leggings and ankle boots, or swap the leggings for a black leather mini skirt and opaque tights. Keep the sweater oversized so the outfit does not feel too tight overall. This one suits dinner, drinks or a night out when a regular jeans-and-sweater look feels too plain.

### 6. Oversized plaid knit with cream wide-leg jeans

Light-colored bottoms stop a heavy sweater from feeling heavy. Pair an oversized grey and navy plaid sweater with cream wide-leg jeans, and finish with loafers or clean white sneakers. Tuck only the front of the sweater so your waist shows a little. The cream also makes darker plaids stand out. Choose this when you want a relaxed outfit that still looks planned.

### 7. Plaid sweater vest over a turtleneck

Layering is where plaid gets interesting. Put a plaid sweater vest over a fitted black or cream turtleneck, then add straight trousers or a pleated skirt. The vest gives you the pattern without the bulk of long sleeves, which helps if you run warm indoors. Brown loafers or tall boots both work. A vest also keeps the outfit light enough for those in-between days in early fall.

### 8. Black and white plaid sweater with black trousers for the office

One reason people avoid plaid at work is the fear of looking too casual. A fitted black and white plaid knit with tailored black trousers fixes that. Add pointed flats or a low block heel and a structured bag. In colder weeks, layer a long camel or grey coat on top. The pattern is clearly there, but the palette is quiet enough for most offices.

### 9. Plaid sweater with a satin midi skirt

To dress a plaid sweater up, pair it with something fluid. A fine-knit plaid sweater tucked into a satin or slip midi skirt gives you texture contrast and a nice shape. Pick a skirt in a color from the plaid, such as olive, burgundy or chocolate. Ankle boots or low kitten heels finish it. This is a good option for dinner or an event where jeans would feel too casual.

When you want plaid without making the whole outfit about it, let it peek out from under a coat. A beige trench over a plaid sweater shows the pattern at the collar and hem only. Olive and forest green plaids look best in an earthy palette. Wear one with medium-wash jeans and camel or tan boots, then add a brown leather belt and a cream beanie if it is cold. ## Styling tips that work with every look

Keep to one main pattern at a time. If you want to mix plaid with stripes, put a thin striped tee under the sweater, keep both in the same color family and make sure the scale of the two patterns is different. Large plaid with fine stripes usually works better than two patterns of the same size.

Repeat one color from the plaid somewhere else. A burgundy line in the sweater can show up again in a bag, a belt or your boots, and the outfit starts to look planned.

Balance volume. When the sweater is big, keep the bottom half slim or straight. When the sweater is cropped or fitted, you can wear wide-leg pants or a fuller skirt.

Use accessories to change the mood. A scarf and beanie make the same sweater feel casual. Gold hoops and a structured bag make it feel dressier.

Think about the coat before you leave. A camel coat, a black leather jacket and a denim jacket all work over plaid. Skip another bold pattern on the outside.

## Plaid sweater outfit FAQs

**What goes with a plaid sweater?** Solid pieces in colors that appear inside the plaid. Dark denim, black trousers, cream jeans and brown bottoms work with most plaids.

**Can I wear a plaid sweater to work?** Yes. Choose a small-scale plaid in a quiet palette such as black and white or grey and navy, and pair it with tailored trousers or a midi skirt.

**How do I keep a plaid sweater from looking bulky?** Pair it with slim or straight bottoms, tuck in just the front, push up the sleeves and choose a V-neck or open cardigan if you want a lighter look.

**What shoes look best with plaid sweaters?** Ankle boots, loafers, knee-high boots and clean white sneakers all work. Match the shoe color to a shade in the sweater when you can.

**Which plaid colors are easiest to wear?** Camel and cream, grey and black, and navy and green. These are the easiest to match with clothes you already own, so start there if you are buying your first plaid sweater.

If you are unsure where to start, begin with outfit 1 or 2. They use pieces most closets already have, and one plaid sweater is enough to make them work.
